## Claude:
I fixed it, so the screen labels no longer move as you orbit. The change is committed (`64956af`) and pushed.

**Cause:** face on, each display's label floats at the top of its pile of windows, so it reads above them. Over the first 20° of a turn it sinks back down to its display. That height is depth in the scene, and it was lifted straight off the plane. So once you turned, the label slid sideways on screen and then slid back as it sank. Under the old rule, the Middle Center label drifted 19 px at 3°, 34 px at 8° and 27 px at 14°, then returned by 20°.

**Fix:** the label now rises along your line of sight, the same way ⌘-raise moves windows. It still sits above the pile face on and sinks as you turn, but it never moves on screen. In `screenmaps.js`, `setCamVars` works out the line of sight from the camera (`--sight-x`, `--sight-y`), and the label's transform in `screenmaps.css` moves along it.

**Checked on quad:** I measured all five labels from 0° to 42°, and each stayed within 0.01 px of its spot on its display. A screenshot at 8° shows the labels in their corners.

I also updated the screenmaps README's note on turning. `data/bear-notes.jsonc` and `AGENTS.md` were already modified when I started, so I left them out of the commit.
