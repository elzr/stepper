**What was wrong:**
- **Names stopped syncing in June.** F010's folder got its `F010-` prefix, but `layout.lua` still pointed at the old path, so every sync failed.
- **The two Samsungs look identical to macOS.** The only difference is a serial number that macOS doesn't expose, so Lunar can't tell them apart on its own.
- **Lunar connects each slider to a monitor at launch using the display IDs it saved last session.** macOS can swap those IDs between the two Samsungs on a reboot or a brief disconnect. Lunar then comes up with Left and Right crossed, and its HDMI LG mapping went wrong too. A plain restart only sometimes fixes it. I reproduced this on purpose by swapping the saved IDs.
- **After the macOS 27 install, the automatic sync silently failed for about 15 minutes.** Homebrew's Python is an Intel build here and needs Rosetta, which wasn't reinstalled until 00:20.

**What F010 does now:**
- **Checks:** names, Lunar's live display IDs, and whether each slider actually reaches the right monitor.
- **When something is off:** it quits Lunar, writes the correct names and IDs, relaunches it, and re-checks. I triggered a crossed state deliberately and it repaired it on its own in about 20 seconds.
- **When it runs:** after any screen change (including brief disconnects), when Hammerspoon starts, and when the screens wake. It skips while displays are asleep, because querying a sleeping monitor can hang.
- **Files:** `lua/layout.lua` and the script and README in `features/F010-sync-display-names-in-Lunar/`.

**Things to know:**
- **Lunar setting:** I turned on "Match DDC port based on the IOKit position" in Lunar's Advanced settings. It helps but isn't enough by itself.
- **Brightness:** Lunar reapplies each monitor's saved brightness at launch. While the sliders were crossed, the two Samsungs got each other's levels, so you may want to readjust them (Left was set to 11 and Right to 7 at the last restart).
- **Python:** I kept your Homebrew Python, as your rules ask. The catch is that F010 depends on Rosetta after future macOS upgrades. If it can't launch, it now logs `[layout.lunar] Error: couldn't launch …` to the Hammerspoon console instead of failing silently.
